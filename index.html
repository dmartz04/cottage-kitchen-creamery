✨ THE PURE FRUIT TREAT

What is Nice Cream?

Nice cream is a wholesome, 100% fruit-based soft serve created by whipping frozen fruit into a rich, luscious dessert with no added sugar or dairy.

Step 1
Start With Real Fruit

Bright strawberries and ripe banana are the foundation for a naturally colorful, fruit-forward treat.

Step 2
Freeze It Cold

Frozen strawberries and banana pieces bring the chill and structure needed for a thick, creamy blend.

Step 3
Bright Neapolitan Nice Cream

Blend the fruit into a silky soft-serve texture, then bring the classic strawberry, vanilla and chocolate colors together.

THE EVERYDAY CHALLENGE

The Homemade Dilemma

Making fruit-based nice cream sounds effortless. In practice, getting consistently ripe fruit, keeping enough frozen fruit on hand, and finding a truly fruit-forward alternative can make the routine surprisingly demanding.

01 · RIPENESS

Perfect Ripeness Is a Moving Target

Fruit can move quickly from perfectly ripe to heavily spotted and soft. That makes it harder to keep the same flavor, color, and texture from one batch to the next.

02 · PREP & STORAGE

Keeping Frozen Fruit Ready Takes Space

Peeling, portioning, freezing, and storing enough fruit for regular nice cream means planning ahead—and dedicating valuable freezer space to the process.

03 · CONSISTENCY

Every Batch Can Be Different

Small changes in fruit ripeness, freezing, and preparation can change the finished texture. The result is a process that takes more attention than the final scoop suggests.

04 · THE ALTERNATIVE

Convenience Often Means a Different Product

Store shelves offer plenty of dairy-free frozen desserts, but they are formulated as packaged frozen desserts—not the fresh, whipped-fruit soft-serve experience people are looking for.
