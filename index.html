Meet Cottage Kitchen Creamery

Welcome to our local cottage food operation, where small-batch passion meets wholesome ingredients. We craft signature drypack nice cream blends designed to eliminate the everyday kitchen hassle of spoiled produce, unpeeled fruit, or missing components when a dessert craving hits.

The Old Routine

Say goodbye to the produce race & freezer clutter

We've all been there: buying mountains of fruit with big intentions, only to watch half of it overripen on the counter. Then comes the weekend chore of peeling, chopping, bagging, and playing Tetris with your crowded freezer—just to realize you're missing a key ingredient right when your sweet tooth strikes.

The Drypack Upgrade

Snap open, blend up, zero waste

Enter the ultimate kitchen flex. With Cottage Kitchen Creamery's shelf-stable drypacks, the struggle is officially over. No spoilage anxiety, no messy prep work, and zero guesswork. Whenever the mood hits, just rip open a pouch, toss it in the blender with your base, and whip up restaurant-quality, dairy-free soft serve in seconds.
